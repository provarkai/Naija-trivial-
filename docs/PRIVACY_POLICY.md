## What we collect

**Account information.** If you choose "Continue with Email", the app
stores the email address you type on your device only (via Android
DataStore). We don't verify this address or use it to contact you — it's
just a display name for your session. Choosing "Continue as Guest" stores
no identifying information at all.

**Content you generate.** Text you type into a generator form (e.g. a
business name, a customer's message, a project scope) is sent to our
server to produce the requested document. Our server forwards it to
[OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/privacy), a third-party AI routing
service, which forwards it to the underlying AI model to generate a
response. Our server does not store this content — it exists only for the
duration of that one request. See OpenRouter's own privacy policy for how
they and their model providers handle this data.

**Generated documents.** The AI outputs you save are stored locally on
your device (not on our servers) and are visible only to you within the
app. You can delete any saved document at any time from the History
screen; deleting the app removes all of them.

**What we don't collect.** No location data, no contacts, no camera or
microphone access, no advertising identifiers, no analytics or crash
reporting SDKs are integrated in this version of the app.

## How we use it

- To generate the documents you ask for.
- To keep you signed in between app launches.
- We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Third parties

- **OpenRouter** (and, transitively, the AI model it routes your request
  to) processes the text you submit to each generator tool, solely to
  return a response to that request.
- We do not use any other third-party service in this version of the app
  (no analytics, ads, or crash reporting).

## Data retention and deletion

- Locally stored documents and account info live on your device until you
  delete them (per-item, or by clearing app data / uninstalling).
- Our server does not retain the content of your requests after responding
  to them.

## Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If how the app handles data changes (for example, adding real
authentication, analytics, or billing), this page will be updated and the
"Last updated" date above will change.
